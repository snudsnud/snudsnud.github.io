***Touching Wires (2024)***[^1]

**found textiles, electronics**


[^1]: Touching Wires was an Honours research project articulating the role of tactility in Human-AI interaction with the [ANU Sound, Music, and Creative Computing Lab](https://charlesmartin.au/lab/).

---

The first iteration of *Touching Wires* took the form of a musical tablecloth for human performance. It debuted on 29 May, 2024 at *Andromeda is Coming and Artificially Intelligent Friends (and human friends)* --- a 60-minute performance of new music for new AI musical instruments featuring Alec Hunter, Charles Martin, Yichen Wang and Sandy Ma.
